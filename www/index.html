Shyla Enterprise

Lead smelters, dealers, carbide, stone, CaCO3, activated carbon, agarbatti aur charcoal powder ki alag alag lead list. Har company par call, WhatsApp, email aur follow-up.

Enquiry jodo (TradeIndia / IndiaMART)

TradeIndia ya IndiaMART ka email / SMS / lead text yahan paste karo (ek saath kai bhi chalenge, beech me khali line rakho). Ya lead export ki CSV paste karo. Naam, number, email, city aur requirement apne aap nikal jayegi.

Auto-sync (auto-reply server)

Google Apps Script setup ke baad uska URL yahan daalo. Naye enquiry app kholte hi aa jayenge, aur jin par server ne reply bhej diya wo "Contact hue" me dikhenge.

Auto backup → Google Drive

Isi URL se roz apne aap poora data (leads, orders, settings) aapke Drive ke "Shyla Backups" folder me save hoga. Last 14 din ke backup rehte hain.
